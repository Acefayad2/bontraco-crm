# Bontraco Acceptable Use Policy — Counsel Review Draft

**Status:** Not approved, not published, and not legal advice. Counsel must review this draft together with Bontraco's actual provider contracts, customer agreement, privacy notice, and launch configuration.

## Purpose

Bontraco helps contractor businesses organize customer work, discover potential business opportunities from permitted sources, and operate an optional AI receptionist. Customers remain responsible for the legality of their data collection, outreach, calling, recording, advertising, and service work.

## Permitted lead collection

Customers may use Bontraco to collect and qualify information that they are authorized to access, including:

- public government records made available for reuse;
- public business listings and licensed provider data used within the provider's terms;
- public webpages that do not require authentication or circumvention;
- customer referrals, web forms, inbound calls, and records supplied with appropriate authority; and
- contact-enrichment data obtained under a contract that permits the intended use.

Every discovered lead must retain its source, discovery time, geography, and enough evidence to review why it was collected. A source listing a phone number or email address does not establish consent for automated outreach.

## Prohibited collection and access

Customers may not use Bontraco to:

- bypass login, paywall, CAPTCHA, robots controls, rate limits, technical restrictions, or platform access controls;
- scrape private or restricted areas of Facebook, Nextdoor, LinkedIn, Reddit, or any other service without a permitted API, license, or written authorization;
- use stolen credentials, session cookies, deceptive accounts, residential proxies intended to evade controls, or compromised systems;
- collect passwords, authentication tokens, financial-account credentials, government identifiers, precise location trails, medical information, or other unnecessary sensitive data;
- target or rank people based on protected characteristics, health conditions, financial distress, immigration status, or other sensitive vulnerability;
- collect data about children or knowingly create a lead for a minor; or
- retain provider content longer than the governing license permits.

## Outreach and communications

Bontraco does not grant permission to contact any person. Customers must maintain channel-specific consent and suppression records and comply with applicable federal, state, local, and industry rules.

- No automated or artificial-voice outbound calling may be enabled from a discovered public lead without documented consent and counsel-approved controls.
- Commercial email must use accurate sender information, non-deceptive subjects, required identification and address information, a working opt-out method, and timely suppression. Business-to-business messages are not categorically outside CAN-SPAM.
- A customer must honor do-not-call, do-not-text, unsubscribe, revocation, and internal suppression requests across every connected provider.
- Customers may not harass, threaten, impersonate, discriminate, send deceptive offers, fabricate urgency, or contact people at unreasonable frequency.
- Purchased or enriched lists may be used only when the supplier contract and applicable law permit the specific channel and purpose.

## AI receptionist use

The AI receptionist may answer inbound calls, collect service details, provide approved administrative information, schedule within configured availability, and transfer to an authorized human. It may not impersonate a human, diagnose emergencies, guarantee pricing or insurance coverage, provide licensed trade advice, or conceal required disclosures.

Recording and transcript storage must remain disabled unless the customer has configured a counsel-approved disclosure and consent process for every applicable jurisdiction. Emergency language must direct the caller to appropriate emergency services or the customer's approved escalation path.

## Security and tenant boundaries

Customers may access only their own workspace and records assigned to their role. They may not probe other workspaces, share accounts, defeat role restrictions, upload malware, abuse signed file URLs, interfere with service operation, or attempt to obtain provider or Bontraco credentials.

## Enforcement

Bontraco may rate-limit, suspend, or disable a source, integration, workspace, or user when necessary to prevent harm, investigate suspected misuse, comply with law, or protect the service. Evidence should be preserved for review, access should be limited to authorized responders, and customer notice should follow the approved incident process unless prohibited by law.
